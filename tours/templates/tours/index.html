About Us

{{ what_we_do.title|default:"Our Story" }}

{{ what_we_do.subtitle|default:"Wild. Exclusive. Unforgettable." }}

At Kaelo Expedition Limited, we design private, expertly guided safaris across East Africa's most spectacular wilderness landscapes. Our journeys combine luxury, adventure, and authentic connection to the wild, with every itinerary thoughtfully tailored to our guests.

We bring together the freedom of intimate mobile tented camps with handpicked lodges and permanent camps, allowing you to experience East Africa in comfort while staying close to nature.

From unforgettable wildlife encounters and exploring remote landscapes to authentic cultural experiences with local communities, every safari is designed to feel personal and immersive. Guests can also enjoy experiences such as hot air ballooning, horseback adventures, fishing, and private wilderness moments.

Above all, we believe travel should leave a positive footprint. Our safaris support wildlife conservation, local communities, and the protection of the landscapes that make East Africa extraordinary.
